## Playback at 2× and faster

[#3](https://github.com/oiuy-retro/podcast-trim/issues/3). Auto encodes at least 96 kbps stereo and 64 kbps mono. The old ladder put spoken shows at 24–48 kbps, which fell apart when played at 2× or faster. A bitrate entered in Options is used as entered.
