# Known issues

Open copies live on the [Issues](https://github.com/oiuy-retro/podcast-trim/issues) tab.

## Playback at 2× and faster

[#3](https://github.com/oiuy-retro/podcast-trim/issues/3). Auto encodes at least 96 kbps stereo and 64 kbps mono. The old ladder put spoken shows at 24–48 kbps, which fell apart when played at 2× or faster. A bitrate entered in Options is used as entered.

## Cut preview text

[#5](https://github.com/oiuy-retro/podcast-trim/issues/5). Check cuts lists each range with the transcript words inside it, so a pasted ad list can be confirmed before Approve and cut. The saved cut list on a finished episode shows those words too.
